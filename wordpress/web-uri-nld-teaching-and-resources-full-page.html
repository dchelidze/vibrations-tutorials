Interactive Courseware · MCE 464 Vibrations

Interactive vibrations tutorials

Eleven browser-based geometric modules ordered chronologically by the MCE 464 Fall 2026 lecture schedule. Each module pairs live mechanical animations and complex-plane or phase-space geometry with reproducible MATLAB code and runs directly in the browser with no installation required.

Part I · Single-DOF free, forced, damped, and transform dynamics (Lectures 5–19)

Module 01 · Lectures 5–7

Viscous free-response root locus and logarithmic decrement

Sweep damping across the semicircular s-plane root locus |s| = ωn, critical collision, and overdamped split; extract damping from noisy ring-downs via multi-cycle semilog regression.

Launch tutorial Free response
Module 02 · Lecture 10

Equilibrium stability, eigenvalues, and phase portraits

Drag across the Poincaré (trace, determinant) plane, visualize Liouville phase-area evolution and Jordan shear, and test Hartman–Grobman limits on nonlinear 1-DOF oscillators.

Launch tutorial Phase portraits
Module 03 · Lectures 11–12

Harmonic forcing, force-polygon geometry, and phase resonance

See steady-state equilibrium as a four-vector force polygon, separate phase resonance from amplitude peak shift on the Nyquist loop, animate beating, and identify damping via half-power bandwidth.

Launch tutorial Forced SDOF
Module 04 · Lectures 13–14

Base excitation, rotating unbalance, and seismic sensors

Unify base isolation, accelerometers, vibrometers, and rotating-unbalance machines inside one complex triangle X = Y + Z, exploring the r = √2 crossover and supercritical CM stabilization.

Launch tutorial Sensors & rotors
Module 05 · Lecture 15

Damping mechanisms in the force–displacement plane

Compare viscous ellipses, structural hysteresis loops, and Coulomb dry-friction parallelograms; inspect cycle dissipation, finite-time stick-slip termination, and equivalent viscous FRFs.

Launch tutorial Damping models
Module 06 · Lecture 18

Periodic forcing, Fourier epicycles, and subharmonic resonance

Animate rotating Fourier epicycles, filter discrete harmonic spectra through the SDOF FRF, inspect Parseval energy shares, and trigger subharmonic resonance peaks at r0 = 1/n.

Launch tutorial Periodic forcing
Module 07 · Lectures 16 & 19

Fourier and Laplace transforms in vibration analysis

See the Fourier transform as the imaginary-axis slice of the 3D Laplace surface |H(σ + iω)|, explore poles, convolution, and all six dimensional FRFs.

Launch tutorial Transforms & FRFs

Part II · Multi-DOF modal geometry, tuned absorbers, and continuous beams (Lectures 20–24)

Module 08 · Lectures 20–21

From coupled energy ellipses to linear normal modes

Explore spring-mass pendula, double pendula, and coupled masses; visualize kinetic and potential energy ellipses, tangency, mass-weighted diagonalization, and modal superposition.

Launch tutorial 2-DOF eigenproblem
Module 09 · Lecture 22

Modal decoupling, forced 2-DOF response, and antiresonance

Visualize mass-weighted orthogonality, selective modal forcing, complex-plane phasor cancellation behind driving-point antiresonance zeros, and Rayleigh versus non-proportional damping.

Launch tutorial Modal analysis
Module 10 · Lecture 23

Tuned vibration absorbers, Den Hartog fixed points, and isolation

Animate dynamic force cancellation, sweep damping across Den Hartog’s invariant fixed points P and Q, balance mass ratio against absorber stroke clearance, and explore the r = √2 crossover.

Launch tutorial Absorbers
Module 11 · Lecture 24

Continuous beams as infinite-DOF modal geometry

Solve transcendental frequency equations graphically across five boundary conditions, animate beam mode shapes and interior nodes, visualize L2 orthogonality lobes, and test nodal suppression.

Launch tutorial Continuous beams
