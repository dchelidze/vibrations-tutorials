How NLD uses Notion and Obsidian

Collaborative Databases & Tracking

Notion

Best for relational databases, experiment logs, grant/paper milestone boards, equipment inventories, and shared lab dashboards. Free Plus plan available with a .edu address.

Open Notion →
Local-First Markdown, LaTeX & Zotero

Obsidian

Stores plain-text Markdown files directly on your machine with fast native LaTeX math, bidirectional links, visual Canvas boards, Git backup compatibility, and tight Zotero literature-note workflows.
