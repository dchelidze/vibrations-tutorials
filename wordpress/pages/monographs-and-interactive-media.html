Open Monograph Draft · October 2026

Nonlinear Time Series Analysis: Experimental Nonlinear Dynamics from Measured Records

By David Chelidze and Joseph P. Cusumano. Covers estimation and spectral analysis of measured records, delay embedding, dimensions and Lyapunov exponents, surrogate data, POD/SOD noise reduction, and phase space warping. Complete MATLAB and Python figure code is open on GitHub.
