Decision Framework

How to evaluate a journal before submitting

01 · Citation Context

Use multiple metrics, not JIF alone

Compare 5-year JIF, Scopus CiteScore, and SCImago SJR within the same subject category. Mechanical engineering journals cite more slowly than biomedical or ML venues, so field-normalized quartiles (Q1/Q2) matter more than raw cross-field numbers.

02 · Time Cost

Check median time to first decision

Most major publishers now publish live median days to first decision and acceptance on each journal homepage. Weigh prestige against graduation and grant timelines—an 8-month desk delay rarely pays off over a faster top-tier specialty venue.

03 · Integrity Screening

Verify indexing and avoid predatory venues

Never submit to unsolicited email invitations without checking Web of Science, Scopus, DOAJ (for open access), and Think. Check. Submit. Verify the editorial board includes active researchers whose papers we actually cite.

NLD Submission & Open-Science Standards

Six steps from complete draft to accepted paper

Step 1 · Internal Review

Clear co-author & director sign-off

Every co-author must read and approve the final PDF before submission. Check vector figures, SI units, notation consistency, and claims against the NLD writing guide.

Step 2 · Authorship & IDs

CRediT roles and ORCID

Maintain an active ORCID linked to URI. Document each author’s contributions using the CRediT taxonomy and include exact funding grant numbers in acknowledgments.

Step 3 · Reproducibility

Archive code and data with a DOI

Release figure-reproducing scripts under nld-uri on GitHub, mint an archival Zenodo DOI upon acceptance, and deposit datasets in DigitalCommons@URI where appropriate.

Step 4 · Preprints & Open Access

Check arXiv & URI OA agreements

Confirm the target journal’s preprint policy before posting to arXiv, and check URI Libraries’ transformative agreements before paying article processing charges (APCs).

Step 5 · Research Integrity

Generative AI disclosure

Disclose any AI assistance per the journal’s policy. Authors remain 100% accountable for every derivation, numerical check, and cited reference—never cite unverified references.

Step 6 · Revision & Rebuttal

Point-by-point response letter

Quote every reviewer comment verbatim, respond calmly and quantitatively, highlight exact manuscript revisions (with line/section citations), and verify all re-plotted figures.
